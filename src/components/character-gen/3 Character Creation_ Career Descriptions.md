**CAREER DESCRIPTIONS**

Each career in this chapter has the following sections:QUALIFICATION

* You must make the required roll to enter a career.  
* If you fail the check, you cannot enter the chosen career this term. You must either submit to the draft or take the Drifter career.  
* If you leave a career, you cannot return to it in the next term, with the exception of the draft and the Drifter career.  
* The Drifter career is always open.  
* See "Changing Assignments" for another exception.

SKILLS AND TRAINING

* Each career has four or five skill tables: Personal Development, Service Skills, Assignment Skills (three sub-tables), Officer, and Advanced Education.  
* In each term, pick one table and roll 1D to determine which skill you increase.  
* You may only roll on the Advanced Education or Commissioned tables if you meet the listed requirement (usually **EDU 8** or higher, or a certain rank).  
* **Skills without a level listed:** You gain the skill at level 1 (or increase its level by \+1 if you already have it).  
  * *Example:* **Vacc Suit** increases your skill by \+1, or gives you level 1 if you have none.  
* **Skills with a level listed:** You gain the skill at that listed level, but only if it is higher than your current level.  
  * *Example:* **Streetwise 1** gives you level 1\. If your skill is already 1 or more, there is no benefit. If you have no skill or level 0, you jump straight to level 1\.  
  * *Example:* **Gambler 0** means you receive level 0, which is only a benefit if you have no Gambler skill.

SKILL LIMITS

* A skill may never be increased beyond **level 4** during Traveller creation.  
* A Traveller may never have a total number of skill levels higher than three times their combined **INT** and **EDU**.

BASIC TRAINING

* **First Career:** Instead of rolling for a skill, you get all skills listed on the Service Skills table at level 0\.  
* **Subsequent Careers:** You may pick any one skill listed in the Service Skills table at level 0\.  
* **Exception (Citizens and Drifters):** They use their appropriate Assignment Skills for basic training.

SURVIVAL

* Each career has a survival roll.  
* If you fail, you immediately roll on the Mishap table, which usually forces you to leave the career.  
* You also lose the Benefit roll for the current term.  
* A natural **2** on the survival roll is always a failure.

EVENTS

* If you pass the survival roll, roll on the career’s Events table to see what happens this term.

ADVANCEMENT

* Each career has an advancement roll; you start at rank 0\.  
* If you succeed, you move to the next higher rank, gain an extra roll on any available skill tables, and get any bonuses listed for the new rank.  
* You may only attempt to advance once per term.  
* **Leaving the Career:** If your advancement roll is equal to or less than the number of terms you have spent in this career, you must leave.  
* **Mandatory Stay:** If you roll a natural **12**, you must continue in this career.

COMMISSION

* Applies only to military careers: Army, Navy, and Marines.  
* Succeeding at a commission roll makes you a rank 1 officer, and you progress on the officer’s rank table.  
* Trying for a commission is optional.  
* You may only attempt a commission in your first term unless your **SOC** is 9 or higher, in which case you may try in any term.  
* A **DM-1** is applied to commission rolls for every term after the first.  
* Bonuses to advancement rolls from events can also be applied to commission rolls.  
* If you gain a commission, you may not roll for advancement in the same term, but you can still roll for advancement if you fail the commission roll.

RANKS AND BONUSES

* You begin at rank 0\.  
* Success on an advancement roll moves you up to the next rank.  
* Ranks are most important for military careers but exist in all careers.  
* Some ranks have bonuses (e.g., extra skills) which are acquired immediately upon attaining the rank.

BENEFITS

* When you leave a career for any reason, you gain material benefits (money, equipment, influence), as described fully on page 46\.

DRIFTERS AND THE DRAFT

* If you are not continuing with an existing career, you may attempt to enlist in one career each term.  
* If you fail to qualify for a new career, you have two options:  
  1. Apply to the **draft** (randomly sent to one of the military services).  
  2. Spend the term as a **Drifter**.  
* A Traveller may only enter the draft once in their lifetime (unless otherwise stated).

THE CONNECTIONS RULE

* **Purpose:** To ensure Travellers know one another before the campaign begins.  
* **Procedure:** If two Travellers agree, an event rolled for one Traveller can involve the other.  
* **Reward:** Both Travellers gain one extra skill of their choice for a successful connection.  
* **Limits:**  
  * Maximum of two free skills from this rule.  
  * Each connection must be with a different Traveller.  
  * You may gain any skill, but cannot raise a skill above **level 3** using this rule, nor may you take the **Jack-of-all-Trades** skill.

TRAVELLER CHANGING CAREERS

* To enter a new career, you need to make the appropriate qualification roll at the start of a new term.  
* If you fail, you must either submit to the draft (if eligible) or become a Drifter.

CHANGING ASSIGNMENTS

* This focuses on a different aspect of an existing career instead of starting a whole new career.  
* **Army, Marine, Navy, Nobility, Rogue, Scholar, or Scout:**  
  * Requires a qualification roll.  
  * If unsuccessful, the Traveller continues in the same assignment without penalty, retaining their rank.  
  * If successful, the Traveller adopts the new assignment and continues in the career, retaining their rank.  
* **Agent, Citizen, Entertainer, and Merchant:**  
  * New assignments are considered to be **new careers**.  
  * Upon voluntarily leaving the career, Benefit rolls are made as normal, and a qualification roll is necessary to enter the new assignment.  
  * If the qualification roll is failed, the Traveller must enter the draft or become a Drifter.  
  * If successful, the career begins afresh with the new assignment, starting at rank 0\.  
  * A change in assignment cannot be attempted if you were ejected from the previous career.
