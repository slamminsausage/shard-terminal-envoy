Tone & Scope: Space battles swing between knife-fight dogfights (≤10 km, visual, fast) and methodical “submarine” engagements across tens of thousands of km with light-speed weapons predicting motion.
Core Structure
Round Length: 6 minutes; each Traveller takes a duty (pilot, captain, engineer, sensors, turret/bay gunner, marine, passenger). One pilot and one captain only; others can stack.
If Close/Adjacent (≤10 km): Use Dogfighting rules (p.174).
Combat Steps (by Initiative each step)
Manoeuvre: Spend Thrust to change range or perform combat manoeuvres.
Attack: Fire weapons / board.
Actions: Repairs, jump prep, launch craft, EW, etc.
Initiative: 2D + Pilot skill + Thrust; add Tactics (naval) Effect (captain/fleet) once at start.
Surprise: Rare; if achieved, surprised ship gets no actions first round.
Range Bands (Space Scale)
Adjacent ≤1 km; Close 1–10 km; Short 11–1,250 km; Medium 1,251–10,000 km; Long 10,001–25,000 km; Very Long 25,001–50,000 km; Distant >50,000 km.
Most fights start Very Long/Distant; weapons often engage at Long/Medium.
Manoeuvre Step
Thrust to Move (per band shift; can bank across rounds): Adjacent 1, Close 1, Short 2, Medium 5, Long 10, Very Long 25, Distant 50.
Closing vs fleeing: add Thrust toward each other; subtract if one flees.
At Close/Adjacent, switch to Dogfighting.
Combat Manoeuvres (1 Thrust each; once per round unless Evasive): Aid Gunners (Pilot chain), Docking (Pilot vs Pilot if opposed, –2 to docking ship), Evasive Action (reaction; see below).
Attack Step
Skill: 2D + Gunner (specialty) + DEX DM.
Common Ranged DMs: Short +1; Long –2; Very Long –4; Distant –6; Pulse Laser +2; Beam Laser +4; +1 per full 1,000 tons of target (max +6).
Pilots firing: Can fire a turret or fixed mount at DM–2.
Sequence: Attacker declares → target may Evasive Action → Gunner check → apply damage/crit.
Damage & Scale
Spacecraft scale weapons vs Ground: DM–2 to hit, damage ×10 (Blast 10); Ground vs Spacecraft: DM+2 to hit, damage ÷10.
Apply attack Effect to damage, then subtract Armour; remaining hits Hull. Hull 0 = wrecked.
Critical Hit trigger: Effect ≥6 and damage penetrates Armour. Severity = Effect – 5. Roll location (2 Sensors … 12 Bridge). Stacking: new Severity or (old+1) higher; at 6+ extra criticals = +6D damage to Hull. Sustained damage: every 10% Hull lost → Severity 1 crit (2D location).
Reactions
Evasive Action (Pilot): Spend unspent Thrust; each point lets you dodge one attack, applying –Pilot skill DM to that attack.
Point Defence (Gunner, laser turret): One attempt/round; Gunner (turret) vs incoming missile salvo at impact; Effect removes missiles (double turret +1, triple +2).
Disperse Sand (Gunner, sandcaster): Gunner (turret) vs laser; on success Armour bonus = 1D + Effect vs that attack; consumes 1 canister. Against boarding party: 8D Ground damage.
Actions Step (Initiative order)
Captain: Improve Initiative (Leadership; Effect applies next round).
Engineer: Jump (hurried Astrogation/Engineer checks, +1 difficulty); Offline System (free Power); Overload Drive (Difficult 10+, +1 Thrust next round; fail Effect ≤–6 = M-drive crit 1; cumulative –2 each retry until maintenance); Overload Plant (Difficult 10+, +10% Power next round; fail Effect ≤–6 = Power crit 1; cumulative –2); Repair System (Average 8+, –Severity DM; +1 cumulative per round on same crit; temporary 1D hours).
Gunner: Reload turret (missile/sand; turret can’t attack that round).
Sensors: Sensor Lock (Electronics/sensors; DM+2 to your ship vs that target until broken); Electronic Warfare (opposed Electronics/comms to jam; or vs missiles: Difficult 10+ to remove missiles; once per salvo per round).
Marines: Boarding if Adjacent (p.175).
Any: Reassignment (swap duty, no other action this round).
Missile Combat (Key Points)
Salvos = all missiles at one target in a round; Smart lost at Adjacent/Close.
Flight time (Thrust ~10): Medium/less immediate; Long 1 round; Very Long 4; Distant 10 (–2 to attack). Inert after 10 rounds.
Detection: Immediate Routine 6+ (or 8+ if firing ship undetected), +1 per 10 missiles (max +6). Retry each round at 8+.
Countermeasures: Electronic Warfare removes missiles (Effect) once/round per salvo; Flee (extend range); Point Defence at impact; Evasive Action applies.
Attack roll: Ignore Gunner skill and range; apply DM +1 per remaining missile; Smart uses higher of missile TL or ship TL; Distant launch –2.
Damage: Roll one missile’s damage, subtract Armour, multiply by Effect (capped by missiles remaining); no Effect add to base damage.
Reload: Missile rack reloads in one round; turret holds 12 missiles; nuclear optional (Radiation, Smart).
Spacecraft Weapons & Mounting (Core)
Beam Laser (Med, 1D, TL10) +4 DM; Pulse Laser (Long, 2D, TL9) +2 DM; Missile Rack (Special, 4D, TL7) Smart; Particle Barbette (V. Long, 4D, TL11) Radiation; Sandcaster (Special, defence).
Turrets: Single/Double/Triple; multiple of same type fire as one attack, +1 per damage die per extra weapon (not missiles). Sandcasters stack +1 negation per extra.
Mounting: Fixed forward vs turret; 1 turret per 100 tons hull.
Crew Duties Recap
Pilot, Captain, Engineer, Sensor Operator, Turret Gunner, Bay Gunner, Marine, Passenger.
Automated via software: Fire Control (gun/assist), repair drones+Auto-Repair (damage control), Intellect+Expert (pilot/engineer/sensors).
Engagement Flow Cheat Sheet
Assign duties & roll Initiative (2D + Pilot + Thrust + Tactics Effect).
Manoeuvre: Spend Thrust to change Range Bands (table) or manoeuvres; bank Thrust for Evasive.
Attack: Declare target, target may Evasive, resolve Gunner attacks with range/weapon DMs, apply damage/crit.
Actions: Repairs, EW, locks, overloads, jump prep, reloads, board, reassign.
Missile tracking: Move salvos toward impact, apply EW each round, point defence at impact.
Repeat until disengage/jump/destroyed/boarded.
Quick Tables (Narrative form)
Range Band Thrust: Adjacent/Close 1; Short 2; Medium 5; Long 10; Very Long 25; Distant 50.
Attack DMs: Short +1; Long –2; Very Long –4; Distant –6; Pulse Laser +2; Beam Laser +4; Target tonnage +1/1,000 tons (max +6).
Damage Scale: Space→Ground: DM–2, damage×10 (Blast 10); Ground→Space: DM+2, damage÷10.
Critical Trigger: Effect ≥6 and damage penetrates Armour; Severity = Effect – 5; location 2–12.
